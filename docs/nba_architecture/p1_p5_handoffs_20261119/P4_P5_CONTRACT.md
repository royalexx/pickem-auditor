amendment. P4 follows independent P3 acceptance; P5 follows independent P4

not 144 distinct forecasts. Unknown participation and eligibility stay unknown.

native P4 control tests exercise the unchanged original physical contracts.

P5 adds a separate research-audit archive, using the existing native warehouse's
exclusive create/write-once storage primitive. It validates all upstream handoffs
against original source/model inputs before archival. The archive preserves
exact P1/P2/P3/P4 wires, raw HTTP bodies and receipts, cohort, raw saved model and
executing reader code. Its manifest binds their exact byte hashes, relative
paths, input/output domains, counts and unknown correction history. Duplicate
identical writes may be idempotent; changed content at an existing object or
record must fail. Reader verification replays from archived copies and refuses
changed code, missing objects, altered bytes or resealed claims. No caller
approval, hash or profile label creates source/model/family admission. P5 accepts
genuine conditional-minute forecasts and explicit abstentions as research
records; it does not turn them into a scoring-ready statistic or outcome bundle.

abstentions. The original 36 P1 candidates and all 540 prior-game observations

history, exact-second played-zero interpretation, genuine participation and

scoring, holdout access, fitting, schedule change, production/Tier promotion,
merge or deployment is authorized by this transport contract.
