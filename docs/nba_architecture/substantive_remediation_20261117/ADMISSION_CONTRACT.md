# Capability admission protocol 1.0.0

A modern official PDF may establish its printed historical content without
establishing original pregame availability, source-bound correction completeness,
external player identities, dated trades, future eligible rosters, model fitting,
or predictive skill. Admissions are capability-specific, not a universal VERIFIED
label. The historical reader admits no training rows, never fills original clocks,
and cannot satisfy a prospective forecast or outcome release certificate.

The new API accepts only a PDF, its actual capture receipt and one of three
content capabilities: official printed duration, box score, or explicit DNP rows.
A fixed code-pinned registry digest must bind exact raw PDF hash, original capture
URL/actual retrieval clock, independently reviewed reader bytes and an immutable
independent review receipt hash. Callers cannot supply or override the root.
No root is installed until independent review. Closed capabilities and returned
missingness remain unchanged even if a caller supplies unsupported fields.

This is the first bounded trust contract, not automatic adoption by P1–P5.
Pregame roster/date/identity/availability and fitted-artifact admissions require
separate precise reviewed trust populations; none are inferred from PDF content.
Original native v1 readers and their fail-closed real modes remain unchanged.
Source acquisition is investigated separately; missing original historical
availability and correction evidence are not promised recoverable.
