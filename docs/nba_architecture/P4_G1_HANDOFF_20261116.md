### Physics / source invariants

1. G/O/J reference exact source snapshot/game and immutable content SHA256, including the same G/O scenario IDs/weights/overtime. J **does not resample minutes or participation**. G0 \`validate_integration\` checks scenario and player identity.
2. O totals: exactly five on court per team, with **240 player-minutes regulation** plus **25 additional team player-minutes for each 5-minute overtime**. WS2 \`lineup_segments\` gives a deterministic clock-time five-player witness and is independently integrated against O minutes; it does **not** imply learned exact substitution sequences.
3. Primitive integer outcomes: made two-point FG ≤ attempts, made threes ≤ three-point attempts, made FT ≤ attempts, \`FGM=2PM+3PM\`, \`FGA=2PA+3PA\`, \`PTS=2×2PM+3×3PM+FTM\`, \`REB=OREB+DREB\`; assists cannot be self-credited, attributed to opponents or exceed eligible made teammate FG; credited rebounds cannot exceed missed-shot/eligible FT rebound opportunities. G0 rejects negative counts and impossible aggregates.
4. Player and team identities are exact namespaced IDs with G0 source roster joins; DNP and played-zero states remain distinct and have zero counted stats. Unknown participation is **not** independently estimated or coerced to certainty by J.
5. All four combinations **PR/PA/RA/PRA** come from identical primitive event scenario draws. \`scenario_pmfs\` uses \`marginal_from_joint\`, preserving original scenario weights, numerical ESS/uncertainty and exact Over/Under/Push complementarity. Independently multiplying over-probabilities or re-drawing stat components is prohibited.
6. Seed stream is SHA-based with component/stage key, and G0 scenario SHA plus parent hashes; timing metrics are excluded from the immutable J hash. Rerun under identical Python/NumPy/SciPy environment to verify byte hashes. Original v0.1 documented that full floating hashes need **not** be bitwise portable across NumPy versions.
7. No source timestamps are synthesized. The P4 S adapter explicitly refuses non-synthetic S rates; future model cutoff, wrong team/player, injected realized minutes, hidden source fields and corrupted SHA/weights must fail closed.
8. The WS5 pooled event kernel provides **aggregate shot-component accounting**, not exact possession-by-possession timing, free-throw context or empirically verified team-possession equality. It can conserve book-score counts without learning every NBA clock/gameplay transition. These approximations are disclosed, not represented as exact basketball tracking.
