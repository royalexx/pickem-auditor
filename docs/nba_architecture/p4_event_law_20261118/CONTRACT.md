# NBA P4 event law proposal 1.0.0

Identifier `NBA-J-CLOCKED-POSSESSION/1.0.0-PROPOSAL`. Phase 4 shadow engineering under PA-V25-MASTER-2026-11-09 / v1.1. Accepted G0 remains pinned to 59d28dc23be97de2a6a82d22be8338151e31f5b6.

## Input and statistical law

One execution retains every supplied G/O draw and weight. The native synthetic adapter validates the exact P2 artifacts and P3 sidecar via the existing pinned readers. S1/S2 final count laws remain immutable comparators. For this new version only, the adapter explicitly extracts their Gamma mixing parameters (counts per minute) and samples independently per player and statistic once per scenario. These samples are *propensity inputs* to event choices. They do not generate separate target counts. S3 supplies its beta conversion parameters. One sampled conversion probability per player is reused for all of that player's realized three-point attempts. Projected attempts and P3 exposure/share fields remain pregame quantities. The S3 projected-attempt predictive law remains a separate comparator. Realized attempts are endogenous, and conditioning on their count or the path can alter conversion. There is no exact conversion PMF preservation claim.

Offensive shooter weights are points propensity × P3 possession share / minutes, with uniform fallback on an all-zero vector. Three-point attempt probability is `min(0.8, 2 * threes propensity / points propensity)` with zero when the denominator is zero. The other field-goal conversion is 0.50. Free-throw conversion is 0.76. Assister and rebounder identity weights use their corresponding S propensity, with uniform fallback. The fixed coefficients are engineering parameters. G score-state and P3 role state operate through the supplied G/O scenario and do not directly alter the event transition probabilities. DNP receives no events. Played-zero is distinct and also receives no events.

G team possessions are projected counts for the entire scenario. The mean of the two team projections divided by game seconds sets the common pace. In a period of length L seconds, there are `2 * round(L * pace)` sequential possession slots, half to each team. Reject if a slot is shorter than six seconds or longer than 24 seconds. Period starters are first team in Q1 and Q4, second in Q2 and Q3, and first in OT by deterministic fixture convention, with symmetric counts. Realized possession counts are these slots. Fixed pacing and round-to-even quantization are part of the engineering law. Per-team deviation from G and projected versus realized attempts are reported. A slot starts at its scheduled boundary, with the next team obtaining control at the end of the slot. Outcomes occur in its closing seconds, with whole-second offsets relative to the slot end. The shot clock resets at control acquisition or an offensive rebound. By construction, each live sequence is shorter than the applicable clock, with an offensive rebound continuation shorter than 14 seconds. Clocks use elapsed game seconds, four 720-second periods and zero to two 300-second OTs. No live attempt is at the horn.

## Event semantics and scope

One slot starts with a turnover (0.12), a two-FT shooting foul (0.12), a retained one-FT defensive three-second penalty (0.02), or a field-goal opportunity (0.74). The shooting-foul trip produces no field-goal attempt. Nonterminal misses and retained dead-ball misses produce no player rebound. A made terminal FT ends control. A missed terminal live FT or a missed field goal has exactly one resolution. The reclaiming side is offense with probability 0.22 and defense otherwise. Given the reclaiming side, 0.08 of resolutions are team/unattributed out-of-bounds with no player credit, otherwise a named player gets the rebound. Offense retains the same possession and gets one final field-goal opportunity in the slot. The final opportunity on a miss resolves to defense, including defensive team recovery, as a bounded transition assumption. The retained penalty always returns to a field-goal opportunity. A made field goal is assisted with probability 0.6. Its named teammate is distinct from the shooter. Free throws have zero clock consumption within a trip and no assists. A trip's shooter must be on the floor at the trip clock; all other credits use their own event clocks. This scope excludes and rejects imported events or assertions for and-ones, three-shot trips, flagrant fouls, substitutions within a trip, jump balls after the tip, lane violations, credited horn rebounds and other unspecified cases.

The original exact O stints are used as half-open intervals with a 1e-7 minute integration budget inherited from G0. No replacement O schedule, sorting of stints, endpoint rounding or clock reconstruction by total minutes. O early-exit cap is a total-minute constraint. A claimed exact injury/exit time is outside this version and rejected. The clock witness represents simulated game time, including retained penalty stoppages. It does not establish an observed rotation. If O changes at a clock in open play, reject a scenario unless the change can occur at a stoppage. Native results must label a separate engineering rotation boundary if this requirement rules out the supplied schedule.

## Conditioning and replay

For a fixed latent realization and unchanged G/O scenario, draw entire paths under the transitions above. Accept only a tie after Q4 and each nonfinal OT when another OT is scheduled, and a non-tie at the final horn. Ordinary quarter scores impose no restriction. Stop at the first violated boundary and retain the complete generated prefix; 128 attempts per fixed latent realization. No latent redraw on a retry. If Z is the conditional acceptance probability, the failure atom is (1−Z)^128, and the law of a returned path at those parameters is the forward law restricted to legal score checkpoints and divided by Z. A run with any exhausted scenario produces no forecast. Every scenario and attempt remains in the receipt. Seeds are keyed by law, game, scenario and master seed. All event-derived primitive and combo totals use a single accepted path.

## Predeclared engineering comparisons

Fixed native fixture: exact pinned P4 latest O2 with n=4 and O seed 17, J seeds 200000–200255. Primitive target is the full exact old fixed-G/O NB mixture and its per-scenario distributions, played conditional where participation is positive and with an explicit zero-count atom for sampled zero-minute/DNP where reporting the fixed sample. Synthetic weights do not establish genuine participation probabilities. For source-comparison acceptance, each primitive mean error must be at most max(0.5 count, 5% of reference mean), KS distance at most 0.05 and absolute zero/tail mass error at most 0.025. These are engineering materiality limits: less than a half count or 5% on the mean scale, five percentage points across integer thresholds and half that at the named tails. Native tail cutoffs are PTS 30/60, REB 12/20, AST 10/20, 3PM 5/20. When reference mass is below 1e-6 the additional requirement is a rigorous absolute error bound of 1e-6 or a finding of unresolved rare-tail equivalence. The sample size is for screening gross misspecification; a confidence interval that crosses a limit means inconclusive. Legacy S supplies no joint probability reference for PR/PA/RA/PRA; an independence convolution is descriptive only. All eight empirical PMFs, means, variances, within-player covariance and zero/tail counts are reported. Structural-zero tails are tested through proof. Exact reference calculations for small finite schedules evaluate transition arithmetic separately from S distortion.

Use one independent cluster per master seed including all of its fixed weighted scenarios. Report per-scenario and pooled estimates with effective independent cluster count, all completion/failure rates and simultaneous 95% bounds using Hoeffding with union bound across declared scalar indicators and integer CDF thresholds. For mean confidence use a bounded-count Hoeffding interval. Report point distances and mean cluster SEs as diagnostics. Weight denominators are the declared fixed scenario sum, or the recorded positive-exposure sum if explicitly reporting a played-positive conditional. Do not publish a success-only PMF after a failed study path. Truncation bounds for old NB computations are distinct from Monte Carlo uncertainty. Calibration, real input admission, predictive skill and G1 are separate gates.

## Migration and rollback
