# NFL 2024 — extended 18-week accuracy and confidence development report

**Period:** 2024 Weeks 1–18. **Scope:** 11 NFL volume markets, archived multi-market model (NOT the new targets-only Phase 2 participation simulator).
**Development-only:** the historical thresholds are synthetic, not verified bookmaker lines; 2024 is already model-design-exposed; unavailable settlement rows abstain, never become zero by assumption.

**Protocol:** market-global Platt logistic fitted only from earlier settled weeks; market-and-position role offsets shrink to zero with weak history; Week 1 is exact raw. All comparisons within a given market and week share identical eligible game-player-line outcomes.

## Season progression: does calibration learn?

| Period | Graded | Raw Brier | Global Brier | Role Brier | Raw ≥80% hit (n) | Global ≥80% hit (n) | Role ≥80% hit (n) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Weeks 1–6 | 11,036 | 0.2488 | 0.2443 | 0.2437 | 65.5% (1,291) | 63.6% (467) | 63.8% (481) |
| Weeks 7–12 | 13,205 | 0.2488 | 0.2415 | 0.2402 | 67.0% (1,189) | 59.0% (61) | 60.7% (61) |
| Weeks 13–18 | 14,568 | 0.2503 | 0.2408 | 0.2398 | 65.1% (1,342) | 80.0% (20) | 87.0% (23) |

Global and role probability calibration both beat raw Brier in **17/18 weeks** (Week 1 exact identity). Role beats global Brier in **14/18 weeks**. However, the role-calibrated ≥80% selection count falls from **481** in Weeks 1–6 to just **23** in Weeks 13–18, while the raw model continues issuing more than 1,000 high-confidence selections per six-week period. High-confidence hit-rate comparisons at those dramatically different coverage levels do not establish better selection skill. Week 1 alone contributes 208/565 (~37%) of all role-calibrated ≥80% selections, although Week 1 uses no learned 2024 calibration.

Raw Brier did not improve from early to late season, while role-calibrated Brier improved. This is consistent with *learning the raw forecast's frequency bias*, not necessarily learning more accurate underlying player opportunity and count distributions.

## How to read this study

1. A positive Brier gain means the calibrated probabilities scored better on identical observations. A negative gain means worse. This does not require or guarantee a rise in 0.5-threshold directional accuracy.
2. A larger hit rate among calibrated ≥80% picks is not automatically an improvement in ranking: the size and identities of the selected subset changed. The raw ranking signal should be tested at the same selection count with separately calibrated probabilities.
3. The archived model's market thresholds are retrospective synthetic, non-bookmaker-verified. These are statistical diagnostics only, not actual props available to bet.
4. The code exercises exact chronological calibration using *only* past weeks' outcomes. However the archive's original pregame acquisition/publication times are not independently certified, and 2024 was previously exposed to development.
5. This evaluation calibrates an existing multi-market archive; it does not extend the new target-specific Phase 2 participation model to all 11 markets. No production gates are authorized.

The accompanying long-format CSV contains all 18×11×3 source-model×3-calibration outcomes (1,782 rows), including full-sample Brier, log loss, 50% directional accuracy, ECE, 70/80/90/95 selected counts/hits, confidence claims, Under counts, and per-week source SHA receipts.
