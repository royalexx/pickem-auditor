## Scope and decision rules

- **NFL** full-game passing attempts, completions, passing yards, passing TDs, interceptions, carries, rushing yards and TDs, targets, receptions, receiving yards and TDs, combined/solo/assisted tackles and sacks. Other provider-offered markets remain in the rejection ledger until mapped to verified contracts.
- **NHL** full-game SOG, hits, blocked shots, goalie saves, points, goals, assists, power-play points and faceoff wins.
- **NBA** full-game points, rebounds, assists, three-pointers, steals, blocks, turnovers, PRA, PA, PR, RA, stocks and minutes. These scopes follow the [registry](../v2/stat_registry.py). Each stable market must have separate coverage and source/settlement evidence. Non-full-game contracts require a separate registered product definition and remain unsupported in this evaluator, with their rejects retained. No-offer and no-game results apply only to the checked source/window.
- Archived book quotes may support a price comparator when compatible, time-matched and two-sided. Pick’em payout/EV and release require original product rules, DNP, push/void treatment, terms, and independent prospective reliability (§§3.3, 6.6, 9). Apply the same distinction to every sport. Phase 0R requires positive genuine pregame evidence and lineage on all certified rows. There is no invented minimum fraction of certified rows.
- Phase 1.5 §7 permits a **limited blocked-by-data diagnostic disposition** for missing authentic factors with a specific instrumentation ticket. Scientific attribution and authorization for a specific allocator remain open. Pure count markets may omit a separate conversion factor only when the registered producer mechanism defines the player count directly as the allocation. Any missing upstream volume, participation or share remains unmeasured.
