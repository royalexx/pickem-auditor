# Participation research dataset contract

`evidence/advance_candidates.json` contains 348 genuinely observed advance candidate/game rows (279 unique ESPN player IDs). It covers every ESPN roster candidate on both teams in all ten registered games. 321 rows have source-bound NBA identities; unresolved records remain. These are advance membership observations. `forecast_freeze_at=null` deliberately prevents their use as registered game-day freezes. There are zero game-day candidate rows, zero outcome rows and zero fitted training rows. NBA-side unresolved records are separately preserved in `identity_final/team_*.json`; they are not silently turned into ESPN candidates.

Every candidate has game/team/player keys; original source URL and raw receipt/hash/path; source name; publication time (null when unknown); actual retrieval time; freeze time; roster/identity evidence; separate legal eligibility, availability and role evidence; 5/10/20 history fields; M0 support; coverage/ambiguity flags; original-contract presence; all abstentions; and empty outcome fields. Advance histories/M0 are null because this audit did not acquire them. Empty availability evidence means unmeasured in this advance table, not healthy/available. A separately retained current injury report is not retroactively merged into these observation rows.

On an actual registered capture, `research.nba_phase8a.dataset.candidate_table` exports the same core feature fields inside the immutable Phase 7 game-day ledger. It includes every accepted WS1 roster candidate, additional rejected source candidates, and unresolved NBA crosswalk records. Prior histories preserve original source-record references; the enclosing WS1 snapshot retains their receipts. Windows of 5/10/20 are **available history tails**, not assertions that complete five/ten/twenty-game universes were acquired. The collector fetches at most ten prior team games. No absent history entry becomes a DNP. Missing M0 support blocks the handoff.

Pregame feature rows are never edited. Postgame data enter only the existing separately retrieved, timestamped Phase 7 settlement records. Join on `(game_id, team_id, player_id)` with the original ledger hash. Preserve PLAYED, EXPLICIT_DNP, UNKNOWN_BOX_STATUS, UNRESOLVED_NOT_LISTED and actual participants outside the original universe. Minutes are reported only with played status. No DNP becomes a losing Under. Contract scoring needs original matching terms. ESPN FINAL parsing is not independent league-native corroboration.

This is a source-observed roster-membership denominator, not verified legal eligibility. P0 uses timely, game-bound official Out evidence only; positive participation remains unknown. P1/P2 are unfitted. There is no sufficient original-candidate historical archive demonstrated by this ticket.

First valid P1 experiment: after denominators and settlement completeness are audited, fit one intercept-only pooled Bernoulli baseline on **earlier complete candidate/game clusters**, explicitly targeting participation conditional on source-observed roster membership if legal eligibility remains unavailable. Keep unresolved/unlisted labels missing, disclose their count and sensitivity bounds, and do not silently exclude DNPs. Preregister the fit boundary, fixed chronological assessment block and minimum useful precision before training. Evaluate Brier/log loss, calibration, false-zero rate and coverage with game/date clustering; compare a frozen training-rate baseline and source-only P0 exclusion policy on the same evaluable population. Ten pilot games are feasibility data, not an adequate guarantee of precision or adoption. Do not use this membership P1 to bypass J2's legal-eligibility gate. P2 follows only after P1's foundation passes review.
