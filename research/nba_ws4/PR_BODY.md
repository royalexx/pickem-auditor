## Problem and result

Historical morning reporting averaged both opposite sides and displayed an
artificial 50% win fraction. This research-only audit preserves the current
production notebook and adds a standalone strict evaluator, chronological
calibration challengers, adversarial controls and NBA integration boundaries.

Independent raw-source replay reproduces 250 NBA contracts, 47 players, five
games and one date. Original finalized 60/40 Brier is 0.2397549100 versus
0.2465624769 for the same 146 contracts' median bookmaker reference. The result
is descriptive; predictive superiority, prospective calibration improvement,
new WS1–WS3 scientific acceptance and pick'em economics remain unproven.
