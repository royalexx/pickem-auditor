## Frozen model distinctions

- **J1 (conservative shared game).** Draw one shared game-pace latent and opposing script latent; draw team plays with Poisson variation, mutually exclusive pass attempts/sacks/rush plays by multinomial, starter attempts as a subset of team passes, eligible WR/TE and receiving RB target categories including unassigned attempts, catches from targets, yardage/TD from catches, RB/QB rush allocations plus unassigned rush, and opponent-plays-conditioned defender solo/assisted credit. Team play volume remains random. Player-supply parameters are *inputs*, not fitted by this prototype.
- **J2 (uncertainty corrected).** Exactly the same source mean parameters, plus Gamma(mean=1, shape=25) game-level volume uncertainty, per-game Gamma-normalized receiver/rush shares (concentration 28), one Beta catch-rate draw per receiver per game (effective sample 35), Beta snap-rate draw per defender per game, early-exit mixture **only if a verified pregame `qb_verified_exit_probability` is provided**, and standardized Student-t(5) signed per-play yardage instead of Gaussian. The nonlinear share normalization and truncated signed yards can move mean forecasts despite preserving the upstream parameter means: J2 must be tested empirically for *mean drift*. It is not a proven pure variance-only ablation.
- **J3 (script sensitivity).** A fixed, predeclared mixture of neutral 50%, pass-heavy underdog 20%, run-heavy favorite 20%, and low tempo 10%. Pass shift applies to the pregame-identified underdog/favorite; sensitivity is also reported conditional on each script. These are research priors, **not fitted sportsbook scenario weights** and are not adjusted after settlement. A real manifest must furnish sources for favorite/underdog and macro script inputs.
- **J4 (dependence diagnostic).** Not a new independently fitted likelihood. Records within-game correlations for QB attempts/WR targets, RB carries/team passes, QB completions/receiver catches, opposing plays/defender tackles and teammate target competition; independent-marginal product is the zero-correlation null. No automatic multileg recommendations. Credible proof requires retrospective joint Energy/Variogram and co-occurrence validation on matched historical outcomes.

## Guaranteed checks and deliberate nonconservation

`plays = pass_attempts + sacks + rush_attempts` for the **internal modeled counted-play universe**, not all NFL playbook events; `0 <= starter_attempts <= team_attempts`; team assigned receiver targets <= team attempts; receptions <= targets; credited player carries <= team rush pool; nonnegative integer count supports; QB completions/yardage/TD depend on starter-origin receiver catches; same-defender `tackles+assists = solo+assists`. Each simulated opponent offensive play offers at most one solo tackle or two **different** assist credits; some credits belong to anonymous untracked defenders. **Combined tackles are not constrained to <= opponent plays** because an assisted tackle can credit multiple defenders. Yardage is signed and not subject to nonnegative count restrictions. The prototype *does not* exactly reproduce official no-play flags, special-teams tackles, multiple passers with real attribution, kneels/spikes, fumbles, half sacks, coverage, official stat corrections or overtime length. These are forecast validity limitations, not invisible data conveniences.

## Numerical contract

For original line `L`, each model uses the same sample to extract `P(Y>L)`, `P(Y<L)`, and `P(Y=L)`. Integer lines retain push mass; fractional lines have zero push for count stats. Each count contract additionally includes a simulation PMF; point and tail quantiles, mean, variance, zero frequency, and binomial Monte Carlo standard errors are stored. Deterministic seed derives from `input_manifest_sha256` (or fixture SHA), frozen event ID, model ID and version. Experiment uses 128 and 512 draws; maximum over-probability MC SE at 512 is about 0.0221, so these draws are **integration checks**, not 1-percentage-point probability estimates. For operationally meaningful threshold precision, allocate draws adaptively from MC error subject to 4,096-draw cap; **abstain** if precision and available compute cannot meet the locked criterion.
